# Future empirical validation plan

CareerCoach's 18/36-item forms are unvalidated vocational-interest explorations, not
clinical or diagnostic instruments. This document is a plan; **no reliability,
validity, fairness, completion-time or outcome estimates have been established**.

1. **Independent content review.** Ask qualified vocational-interest/assessment
   specialists and Malaysian career advisers to review dimension/facet assignment,
   relevance, cultural accessibility, double-barrelled wording and potential prestige
   or gender cues. Record reviewer rationale and disagreements by stable item code.
   Implementation review in this phase is not a substitute for independent expertise.
2. **Student comprehension.** Conduct consented cognitive interviews with Malaysian
   university students and recent graduates across fields and English-language comfort.
   Ask participants to explain activities and response labels in their own words.
   Check that they answer enjoyment, not confidence, opportunity or perceived employability.
   Assess assistive-technology and mobile use, and measure actual completion times.
3. **Consented pilot.** Establish purpose, participant information, withdrawal/deletion
   rules and an appropriate research/ethics process before collecting data. Separate
   research consent from product access, minimize identifying information, and restrict
   access to user-linked sessions. No research export or consent system is implemented
   here; product use is not automatically research participation.
4. **Describe response behavior.** Inspect per-item response distributions, missingness,
   floor/ceiling patterns, response times (if collected with consent), mode effects,
   order/position effects, straight-lining, acquiescence, social desirability, and the
   possibility that unfamiliar activities are interpreted as inability. Similar-looking
   responses alone are not grounds for labeling a participant dishonest.
5. **Evaluate consistency appropriately.** With a defensible sample and analytic plan,
   estimate dimension consistency with uncertainty and methods suitable for ordinal
   responses. Short three-item scales require particular caution. Do not assume a
   published alpha/omega threshold automatically establishes validity or replace content
   review by deleting items solely to increase a coefficient.
6. **Test–retest study.** Use the same bank/mode/item set over a justified interval,
   examine dimensional stability and Holland-code instability around ties, and assess
   the influence of memory and genuine interest development. Do not interpret the
   current comparison page as evidence of meaningful longitudinal change.
7. **Construct validity.** Investigate dimensional structure and expected relationships,
   using an appropriate exploratory/confirmatory analysis plan and held-out evidence
   where feasible. Compare with established licensed measures only with permission and
   appropriate consent. Investigate convergence/divergence rather than assuming every
   RIASEC-labeled inventory is equivalent.
8. **Potential bias and fairness.** Examine coverage and response processes across
   relevant groups only where lawful, ethical and sufficiently supported by data.
   Differential item functioning requires adequate samples and specialist analysis;
   do not fabricate group equivalence, fairness metrics or unsupported group adjustments.
9. **Revise and version.** Document evidence per item, replace or retire problematic
   activities in a new bank version, repeat comprehension work and replication checks,
   and explicitly decide which versions/modes can be compared. Keep historical responses
   and algorithm identifiers. Report limitations, sampling uncertainty and null findings.

No Item Response Theory calibration or computerized adaptive testing is implemented.
Those require an empirical item bank and an appropriate expert-led design. No occupation
RIASEC profiles, career-success probabilities, clinical claims or validation results
are inferred from the software tests.

Primary methodological reference:
[O*NET Interest Profiler Technical Manual (2021)](https://www.onetcenter.org/reports/IP_Manual.html).
It illustrates the scope of item development and reliability/validity research for a
different instrument. CareerCoach does not copy that instrument, claim its endorsement,
or transfer its psychometric evidence to these original items.
